# Visuels d’ambiance (version 1) : prompts de génération

Trois illustrations ont été produites avec l’outil intégré ImageGen lors de la première version, puis converties en WebP (`assets/ambiance/`). Ce sont des illustrations, pas des photos de réalisations.

## industry-hero.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium French industrial safety consultancy portfolio website hero. Generate ONE beautiful editorial architectural photograph of a modern spotless industrial facility circulation corridor, with a clearly defined deep green pedestrian walkway and crisp white directional forward arrows receding into the center distance. Light grey resin floor, slate blue structural beams, translucent roof skylights, cream white walls, yellow and black hazard markings around a closed technical room at the right, a green exit sign in the distance. Architectural symmetry, a slightly elevated viewpoint, strong cinematic natural daylight and geometric shadows, sophisticated understated photography with subtle analog grain, realistic tactile materials. Frame vertical 4:5 or square, rich detail, calm professional premium industrial magazine style. No people, no logos, no brand names, no added typography, no UI, no watermark. The green walkway is the striking focal point and leads the eye towards daylight. This is an illustrative concept, not a documentation of any actual client site.

## bureaux.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: illustrative photograph for French professional industrial safety signage portfolio. Create one sophisticated close-up architectural photograph of a premium rectangular office door plaque mounted on an ivory plaster wall beside a dark wood office doorway. The plaque is minimalist, navy blue top strip and brushed white aluminium lower area, with exact French words 'DIRECTION' in navy uppercase and small number '01'. Small abstract directional arrow. A second similar plaque is softly out of focus in the corridor beyond. Elegant soft daylight from the left, tactile matte textures, subtle shadows, editorial commercial photography, square format. Palette ivory warm gray navy and tiny deep green accent. No logos, no brand names, no extra text, no watermark. Compose plaque in central right area with spacious surroundings. This is a concept illustration not an actual client project.

## locaux.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: illustrative photo for French industrial safety signage portfolio. One premium editorial architectural photograph showing a realistic closed charcoal grey electrical room door inside a light industrial facility, close enough to clearly see a crisp yellow triangular black lightning bolt warning sign, a small red circular no-entry symbol and a professional white door sign reading exactly 'LOCAL ÉLECTRIQUE' in dark navy sans-serif capitals. Well proportioned high quality printed signage mounted at eye level. Adjacent grey concrete wall and neat conduit. Calm natural angled daylight, cinematic but clean, tactile materials, sophisticated industrial photography, square image. Avoid other text, no brand logo, no watermark, no people, no collage. This is an illustrative concept not an actual client site.
