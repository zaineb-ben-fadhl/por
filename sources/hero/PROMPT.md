## Prompt de génération initiale

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: bespoke photographic hero image for KeySafe Training & Consulting, a Tunisian industrial safety signage portfolio website.
Primary request: an attractive, premium and believable safety consulting scene which makes an industrial client want a clear, well organised workplace.
Scene: immaculate contemporary industrial facility, brushed concrete floor, deep muted indigo structural columns, soft daylight through high windows, tasteful green painted pedestrian walkway leading into depth, subtle yellow safety markings, modern machinery far in the background.
Subject: on the right half, two adult North African safety professionals (a woman safety consultant and a male site manager) wearing white hard hats, clear safety glasses, lime reflective vests over navy workwear and work shoes. They are seen in a candid three-quarter side view at a respectful distance, the woman with a tablet, the man gesturing naturally toward a well-organised wall-mounted safety information display on the left half. Professional, calm, reassuring, natural proportions and convincing hands.
The display is a high-quality metal framed evacuation map with simple schematic lines plus three neatly arranged safety pictograms: blue mandatory helmet, yellow warning triangle, green exit. These signs are environmental context, not an instructional safety plan. Keep them visually simple with NO small generated text.
Style: polished architectural/editorial commercial photography, beautifully lit, sophisticated rather than stock-photo poses, realistic materials, crisp foreground and soft atmospheric depth. No staged handshake, no one looking at camera.
Composition: landscape 3:2 image, balanced medium-wide architectural shot. Safety display and people occupy the central 75 percent so it also works within a square-ish website crop. Keep the bottom 15 percent visually simple floor for a separate HTML overlay. Camera at chest height, straight architecture.
Palette: rich midnight indigo and slate, restrained leaf-green accents, warm natural daylight, neutral whites, subtle yellow. Attractive high contrast, not overly dark.
Constraints: no logos, no watermarks, no typography or promotional text, no page UI, no decorative badges, no floating graphic overlays. Render just the photograph-style illustrative scene.
